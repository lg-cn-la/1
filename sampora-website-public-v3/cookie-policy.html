What this policy covers

This Cookie Policy explains how Sampora uses cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar browser technologies on getsampora.com.

The Sampora public website uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, SaleSmartly, and local website storage as part of the current public-site strategy.

Types of Cookies We Use

The Sampora public website may use the following types of cookies or similar technologies:

1. Necessary cookies
These support basic website operation, security, language display, form submission, and local website settings.

2. Preference cookies
These help remember language, interface choices, or other website preferences.

3. Analytics cookies
These help us understand website visits and usage, including page views, traffic sources, clicks, scrolling behavior, heatmaps, session replay, and conversion paths. These cookies help us improve website content, user experience, and website conversion performance.

Analytics tools we may use include:

  • Google Tag Manager
  • Google Analytics 4
  • Microsoft Clarity

These tools may use cookies or similar identifiers including, but not limited to:

  • _ga
  • _ga_*
  • _clck
  • _clsk

Cookie names, duration, and purposes may change when Google or Microsoft updates their services.

Cookie management

Users can block, restrict, or delete cookies through their browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions or analytics accuracy.

Updates and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy if our website technologies, analytics tools, or related practices change. For questions, contact sales@getsampora.com.

Effective date: May 24, 2026

我们使用的 Cookie 类型

Sampora 官网可能使用以下类型的 Cookie 或类似技术：

1. 必要 Cookie
用于网站基础运行、安全、语言显示、表单提交和 Cookie/本地设置保存等功能。

2. 偏好 Cookie
用于记住用户的语言、界面选择或其他网站偏好设置。

3. 分析 Cookie
用于了解网站访问和使用情况，包括页面访问量、访问来源、点击、滚动、热力图、会话回放和转化路径。这些 Cookie 帮助我们优化网站内容、改进用户体验并分析官网转化效果。

我们使用的分析工具可能包括：

  • Google Tag Manager
  • Google Analytics 4
  • Microsoft Clarity

这些工具可能使用的 Cookie 或类似标识包括但不限于：

  • _ga
  • _ga_*
  • _clck
  • _clsk

具体 Cookie 名称、有效期和用途可能会因 Google 或 Microsoft 的服务更新而变化。

Cookie 管理

用户可以通过浏览器设置阻止、限制或删除 Cookie。请注意，禁用某些 Cookie 可能会影响网站的部分功能或分析准确性。

如果我们的网站技术、分析工具或相关实践发生变化，可能会更新本 Cookie 政策。如有问题，请联系 sales@getsampora.com。
